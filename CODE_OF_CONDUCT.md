# Code of Conduct

GoalAnchor welcomes respectful, constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and discuss ideas, evidence, and code rather than attacking people.
- Give feedback that is specific, relevant, and useful to the project.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and intentionally disruptive conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [chenyuyu6666@gmail.com](mailto:chenyuyu6666@gmail.com). The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep project spaces safe and constructive.
